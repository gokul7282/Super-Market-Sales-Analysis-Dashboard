🛒 Supermarket Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supermarket sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand overall sales performance, product category contributions, outlet performance, and statistical patterns.

The objective is to transform raw supermarket data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, Pivot Tables, charts, and statistical analysis.

🎯 Project Objectives

1. Overall Sales Performance
- Analyze total sales revenue.
- Calculate average sales per record.
- Evaluate average product visibility.

2. Product Category and Sales Comparison
- Compare the sales contribution of the top four product categories against the remaining categories.
- Identify the highest and lowest sales-generating product categories.
- Compare total sales across different outlet types.

3. Outlet Performance and Sales Relationships
- Compare Low Fat and Regular product sales across outlet types.
- Analyze the relationship between item visibility and sales.
- Evaluate product category sales contributions across different outlet types.

4. Statistical Analysis of Sales
- Calculate average, median, minimum, and maximum sales.
- Measure sales variation using variance and standard deviation.
- Identify potential sales outliers using Box & Whisker analysis.
- Compare average sales across product categories, outlet types, and outlet locations.

 📊 Dataset Information

- **Dataset:** BlinkIT Grocery Data
- **Total Records:** 8,523
- **Total Columns:** 12
- **Data Type:** Supermarket / Grocery Sales Data

The dataset contains information about product categories, item visibility, sales, outlet types, outlet locations, and other product and outlet attributes.

📈 Dashboard Highlights

The project contains four interactive dashboard sections:

1. **Overall Sales Performance Dashboard** – Displays total sales, average sales, and average item visibility.
2. **Product Category and Sales Comparison Dashboard** – Highlights category contributions, top-performing categories, and outlet sales comparisons.
3. **Outlet Performance and Sales Dashboard** – Compares Low Fat and Regular sales, product visibility versus sales, and category performance across outlets.
4. **Statistical Analysis of Sales Dashboard** – Presents statistical KPIs, sales distribution, Box & Whisker analysis, and average sales comparisons.

🔍 Key Business Insights

- The supermarket generated approximately ₹12.02 lakh in total sales.
- Average sales per record were approximately ₹140.99.
- The top four product categories contributed approximately 50.60% of total sales.
- Fruits and Vegetables generated the highest total sales, while Seafood generated the lowest.
- Supermarket Type1 contributed approximately 65.54% of total sales.
- Low Fat products generated higher sales than Regular products across all four outlet types.
- Item visibility and sales showed almost no linear correlation.
- Household products recorded the highest average sales among the displayed product categories.
- Sales variance and standard deviation helped measure variation across product records.

💡 Business Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing product categories.
- Improve promotional strategies for lower-performing categories.
- Analyze customer demand and preferences to improve product assortment.
- Study successful sales strategies from high-performing outlet types.
- Improve product placement and monitor its impact on sales.
- Use statistical analysis to monitor sales variation and identify unusual records.
